# FinTech Research Portfolio — Sangamitra Chandrasekaran

MSc Financial Technology, Warwick Business School (2026). My research asks one question: **what makes new financial technology trustworthy enough to use?** These projects show how I approach it, through risk modelling, verifiable AI and careful evaluation.

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sangamitra-chandrasekaran)

## Projects

| # | Project | What it shows | Type |
|---|---------|---------------|------|
| 01 | [Wise plc tail-risk analysis](01-wise-tail-risk-analysis/) | How an investigation into €500m of alleged money laundering changed a fintech's downside risk: 4 VaR / CVaR models, GARCH, Kupiec backtesting, event study | Individual |
| 02 | [Verifiable AI for UK fiscal policy (RAG)](02-uk-fiscal-policy-rag/) | A retrieval-augmented AI over 20 think-tank and OBR publications, with hybrid retrieval, reranking and page-level citations | Individual |
